Cuba

A little about Cuba

Cuba is the largest island in the Caribbean, celebrated for its Afro-Spanish culture, revolutionary history, and classic 1950s aesthetic. Led by the Communist Party since Fidel Castro’s 1959 revolution, its capital is Havana and its official language is Spanish. The island is world-famous for salsa music, vintage American cars, hand-rolled cigars, and aged rum. Cuba’s history and cuisine are deeply intertwined, shaped by centuries of indigenous Taíno, Spanish colonial, and West African influences.

Top 3 things to do in Cuba

  1. Old Havana: Walk the historic plazas and ride a 1950s classic car along the Malecón.

  2. Viñales Valley: Tour tobacco farms and dramatic limestone hills.

  3. Trinidad: Enjoy pastel colonial streets and live music
